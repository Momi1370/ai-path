**What it teaches.** Why the update rule makes sense, using the slope of the curve.

**Key points**
- Take a one-parameter cost curve. The slope at a point is positive if the tangent points up to the right. Then w minus alpha times a positive number makes w smaller, which moves left, downhill.
- If the slope is negative, subtracting a negative number makes w bigger, moving right, again downhill.
- Either way w moves toward the minimum. Next question: how to choose alpha, and what happens when it is too small or too large.

**Remember.** The sign of the slope tells you the direction; alpha tells you the step size.
