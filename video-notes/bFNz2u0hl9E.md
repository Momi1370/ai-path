**What it teaches.** The same cost function with both w and b, drawn two ways.

**Key points**
- With two parameters, J(w, b) is a 3D surface shaped like a bowl or a hammock. Each point on the surface is one choice of w and b.
- The same function can be drawn as a contour plot, like a topographic map: every ring joins points with the same cost, and the minimum is at the centre of the rings.
- The contour plot shows the same information in 2D, which is easier to draw and read.

**Remember.** Bowl = 3D picture, rings = the same bowl seen from above.
