**What it teaches.** A big-picture tour of machine learning for people who build with AI. It is the overview video of session 1: watch it first, then go to the details.

**Points the notes take from it**
- "Learning" means searching for the parameters that fit the examples best. For a straight line these are the slope and the intercept.
- Machine learning is only as good as its data: both quantity (too few examples lead to overfitting) and quality matter.
- Neural networks learn their own features. In an image network the first layers detect edges, the next combine edges into eyes and whiskers, and the last decides "cat". Nobody programs the whiskers in.
- Training uses small batches of examples at each step (8 to 32 in the video's example) and an optimizer such as Adam.

**Remember.** Parameters are learned from data, and the quality of the data limits the quality of the model.
