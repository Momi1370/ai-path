**What it teaches.** What supervised learning is, and its first kind, regression.

**Key points**
- Supervised learning learns a mapping from an input X to an output Y. You give the algorithm examples that include the right answers (the labels).
- After learning, the model takes a new input it has never seen and produces the output.
- Examples: spam filtering (email to spam or not), speech recognition (audio to text), machine translation, online advertising (ad and user to click or not), self-driving cars.
- Worked example: predict a house price from its size. A straight line may fit, or a curve may fit better. The prediction for a new house is read off the fitted line.
- Regression means predicting a number out of infinitely many possible numbers, such as 150,000 or 183,000.

**Remember.** Supervised = inputs with the right answers; regression = the answer is a number.
