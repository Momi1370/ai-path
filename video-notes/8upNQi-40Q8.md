**What it teaches.** Underfitting and overfitting, for regression and classification.

**Key points**
- Underfit: a straight line through house-price data that clearly curves. The model is too simple and has high bias.
- Just right: a curve that fits the training data and should also fit new houses (it generalizes).
- Overfit: a high-order polynomial that passes through every training point, almost too well. It will not generalize to new examples and has high variance.
- The same happens in classification: a decision boundary that is too simple underfits, one that twists around every point overfits.
- Next video: how to fix overfitting.

**Remember.** Generalization is the goal: do well on examples the model has never seen.
