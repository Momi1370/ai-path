**What it teaches.** Watching the algorithm run.

**Key points**
- The demonstration starts with w and b at zero (or another guess) and shows the line, the contour plot and the surface together. Every step moves the point toward the centre of the rings, and the line fits the data better.
- This version uses all the training examples at every step; it is called batch gradient descent. Other versions use smaller subsets per step.
- Once trained, the model can predict, for example, the price of a house from its size.
- Next week: several features, non-linear curves and practical tips.

**Remember.** Batch gradient descent = every step looks at the whole training set.
