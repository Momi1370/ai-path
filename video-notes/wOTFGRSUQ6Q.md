**What it teaches.** Which maths to learn for machine learning, and how to learn it so it sticks.

**Key points**
- The speaker, a data scientist with about 10 years of teaching experience, says that understanding the maths separates people who do well from those who struggle.
- Four areas: statistics (the most important, because it stops you picking the wrong algorithm), probability, linear algebra, and calculus (needed mainly for neural networks).
- Do not just accept a formula. Ask what the point of it is and write it out in plain words. For linear regression, "best" line means the one with the smallest total squared distance to the points.
- Topics to cover include the normal distribution, standard deviation, z-scores, hypothesis testing, sensitivity and specificity with confusion matrices, conditional probability and Bayes' theorem, vectors and matrices.

**Remember.** For every formula, say in one sentence what it is trying to do.
