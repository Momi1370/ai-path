**What it teaches.** Why many tasks cannot be programmed by writing rules, and why machine learning therefore matters across industries.

**Key points**
- Tasks such as web search, speech recognition, reading x-rays or driving a car cannot be written down as explicit rules, so the computer has to learn them from examples.
- The course teaches the main algorithms and also the practical know-how for making them work.
- A study quoted in the video estimates that AI will add about 13 trillion US dollars of value a year by 2030, and far more value is still untapped.
- Ng mentions the quest for AGI (human-level intelligence), which comes back later in the course.

**Remember.** Learn from examples when rules are too hard to write.
