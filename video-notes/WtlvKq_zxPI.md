**What it teaches.** The idea of gradient descent: a systematic way to find the parameters with the smallest cost.

**Key points**
- Gradient descent is used for linear regression and for training some of the biggest models in AI. It works on any cost function, with any number of parameters.
- Start with a guess (for example w = 0, b = 0).
- Repeat: look around, take a small step in the direction that goes downhill fastest, and stop when you reach the bottom.
- Analogy: you stand on a hill and want to reach the lowest valley by always stepping downhill.
- On a surface with several valleys, where you start decides which local minimum you reach.

**Remember.** Small downhill steps, repeated until you stop going down.
