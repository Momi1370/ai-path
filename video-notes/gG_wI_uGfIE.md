**What it teaches.** Unsupervised learning, and its best-known kind, clustering.

**Key points**
- In unsupervised learning the data has inputs but no labels. The algorithm is not told the right answer; it must find structure on its own.
- Clustering groups similar points together.
- Examples in the video: Google News grouping related articles about the same story, grouping people by their DNA activity, and grouping customers into market segments.

**Remember.** No labels, no right answer given: the algorithm discovers the groups.
