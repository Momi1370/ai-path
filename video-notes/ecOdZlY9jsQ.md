**What it teaches.** Creating better input features from the ones you have.

**Key points**
- The choice of features often decides how well a model works.
- Example: a house plot has frontage x1 and depth x2. The area x3 = x1 times x2 may predict price better than either alone.
- Using knowledge of the problem to design new features by transforming or combining the originals is called feature engineering.
- One flavour of this lets a linear model fit curves, shown in the next video.

**Remember.** A good feature is often a product, ratio or other combination of the ones you were given.
