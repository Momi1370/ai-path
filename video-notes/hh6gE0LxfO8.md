**What it teaches.** The second kind of supervised learning: classification.

**Key points**
- Classification predicts a category out of a small set of possible outputs. Categories can be numbers (0 or 1) or words (cat, dog).
- Example: is a breast tumour benign (0) or malignant (1), from tumour size. The task has only two possible outputs, which is why it differs from regression.
- With two features (tumour size and patient age) the points live in a plane, and the algorithm finds a boundary that separates the two classes.
- Output class and output category mean the same thing.

**Remember.** Regression predicts any number; classification picks from a small list of categories.
