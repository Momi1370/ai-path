**What it teaches.** A definition of machine learning, and the two main types.

**Key points**
- Arthur Samuel's definition: the field of study that gives computers the ability to learn without being explicitly programmed.
- Samuel's checkers program played thousands of games against itself and became a better player than Samuel. More games means more experience, which means a better player.
- Supervised learning is the type used most in real applications and advancing fastest. Unsupervised learning is the other main type. The first two courses of the specialization focus on supervised learning.
- Knowing the algorithms is not enough: knowing how to apply them effectively is what separates good teams from teams that waste months.

**Remember.** More data and experience should make the program better, without anyone rewriting its rules.
